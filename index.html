CampusTap

Offline-First Campus Payments

NFC & barcode ID payments with smart pre-ordering, so students spend breaks, not time in queues.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Campus life runs on waiting

Long service queues

Students waste 20 to 30 minutes of a short break standing in lines at the canteen, photocopy shops and stationery stalls.

Connectivity dead zones

Basements, thick-walled lecture halls, auditoriums and hostel corners have poor signal, so online payment apps fail when students need them most.

Cash and change friction

Carrying cash and dealing with loose change slows every transaction and causes accounting friction for campus vendors.

PROPOSED SOLUTION

Tap. Pay. Pick up.

Offline NFC / barcode ID payments

ID cards carry an NFC chip or dynamic barcode with a cryptographically signed token. No cellular data or Wi-Fi needed on the student's phone.

Smart in-app pre-ordering

Order food, snacks or printouts from anywhere on campus, quick-commerce style, and pick up the moment they are ready.

Wallet top-ups via UPI

Parents or students reload the digital campus wallet anytime through the college web portal or app.

Transparent multi-user tracking

Students & parents get live spending history and budget oversight. College management gets ledger audits and vendor analytics.

TECHNICAL APPROACH

A simple, proven stack

Student Interface

PWA or mobile app in React / Flutter to check wallet balance, order status and transaction history.

Merchant POS Terminal

Web-based or lightweight app at shop counters on shop Wi-Fi, with NFC readers and barcode scanners.

Security & Encryption

Time-sensitive encrypted hashes (signed tokens) on ID cards prevent cloning, duplication and tampering.

Backend & Database

Node.js / Express or Python server with MongoDB / PostgreSQL for accounts, real-time ledger and UPI callbacks.

ARCHITECTURE DIAGRAM

Hybrid offline / online

1

Offline tap / scan

Student ID is tapped or scanned at the merchant POS.

2

POS verification

Shop internet verifies identity, checks balance and logs the transaction.

3

Central server & cloud DB

Syncs the student wallet, vendor kitchen screen and admin analytics.

4

UPI gateway

Used only for wallet recharge, when internet is available.
